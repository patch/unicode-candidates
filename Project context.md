## Current objective

Develop an open, evidence-led research programme for prospective Unicode characters and repertoires. Each candidate may be supported, opposed, deferred, or left unresolved; the project is not an advocacy list and does not imply official Unicode candidate status.

Initial parallel work prioritizes Durian, All Gender Symbol, and Urinal, with Inverted Cross as another near-term project. The existing mature research corpus belongs to [[Candidates/Durian/Overview|Durian]].

## Scope

The project’s central question must concern whether one or more new characters should be encoded. Possible emoji treatment of those characters remains an associated but distinct track. Standalone proposals about CLDR data, Unicode Standard Annexes, character properties, or other non-character technical changes remain outside this repository. Those projects share some process and writing practices, but their substantive questions, evidence, and review routes differ enough that including them would weaken the repository’s organizing principle. Reassess this boundary only if sustained work creates a concrete need.

Encoded former candidates and other existing characters may be studied as comparative or process precedents. Cross-candidate findings belong in `Research/`; repeatable procedures and refinement logs belong in `Methods/`.

## Candidate organization

`Candidates.md` is the authoritative public inventory. Candidate folders are created only when material exists. An independently viable symbol retains its own project even when it shares a study or prospective submission with another symbol; this applies particularly to All Gender Symbol and Urinal.

The internal lifecycle is defined at [[Candidate lifecycle]]. Official statuses and dated submission events are recorded separately for each character-encoding or emoji track.

## Public development

The repository is intended for open access on GitHub. Public access may precede formal contribution documentation. Original project material is intended for CC BY-SA 4.0 publication, while third-party reference material remains subject to its own rights and publication review.

## Inventory provenance

On 23 July 2026, the candidate inventory was reconciled with the project owner’s private `Unicode Proposals` index without opening its linked private notes or resources. Its two non-character technical-project ideas were not imported because they fall outside the scope above. The inventory also preserves subjects from the 2015–2016 `patch/unicode-candidates` repository.
