# Next Research

Run an approval-gated, bounded research cycle. Apply the project-wide principles in `AGENTS.md` throughout.

## Select the task

1. Read the project context and inspect outstanding tasks, research-lead sections, unresolved claims, negative findings, and any ranked future-research list across the vault. Use local evidence before making limited viability checks elsewhere.
2. Exclude candidates that require unavailable private material, fieldwork, paid acquisition, external contact, a material user decision, or authority not supplied by the user. Treat those as future tasks rather than actionable now.
3. Rank the remaining candidates by:
   - importance to the character-encoding case, including capacity to weaken it;
   - ability to resolve a consequential uncertainty or counterargument;
   - dependency-unlocking value;
   - time sensitivity; and
   - likely evidential return within a bounded pass.
4. Recommend exactly one task. Do not begin substantive research before approval.

Present:

- the proposed research question;
- why it outranks the nearest alternatives;
- a high-level method and intended project outputs;
- principal uncertainties and counterarguments;
- the limits of the bounded pass; and
- a request for approval or optional changes.

Keep this proposal compact enough for a user with limited capacity. If the command includes an explicit approval of a previously proposed task, proceed directly to execution instead of selecting again.

## Execute after approval

Research the approved question and directly material evidential branches. Follow useful evidence without seeking permission for every search, but:

- allow at most one material methodological reframing and record why it was necessary;
- do not turn an adjacent lead into a separate full investigation;
- stop when the planned questions are answered, the next step requires unavailable evidence or new authority, or further searching produces marginal repetition; and
- add a materially different lead to future research instead of expanding the approved task.

Apply the standing evidence, provenance, rights, and research-neutrality rules from `AGENTS.md` rather than restating them here.

## Integrate the result

Before reporting back:

1. Integrate findings into the appropriate project notes and source records, updating affected summaries, data, and review dates where necessary.
2. Record reusable methodological lessons separately when warranted by `AGENTS.md`.
3. Re-rank the canonical project-wide future-research list when one exists. Otherwise update the relevant ranked listing and local lead sections without creating competing project-wide rankings.
4. Check internal links and inspect the final diff.

In the final handoff, lead with the research conclusion. Identify material reframing, important limitations, the effect on the encoding case, files changed, and any newly prioritized lead.
