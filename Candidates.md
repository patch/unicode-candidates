This is a research inventory, not an official Unicode candidate list and not a judgement that every subject should be encoded. Each entry may eventually support a proposal, oppose one, or remain unresolved. Internal phases follow [[Candidate lifecycle]]; exact official statuses are recorded separately for each submission track.
