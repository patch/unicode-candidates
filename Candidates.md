This is a research inventory, not an official Unicode candidate list and not a judgement that every subject should be encoded. Each entry may eventually support a proposal, oppose one, or remain unresolved. Internal phases follow [Candidate lifecycle](<Project/Candidate lifecycle.md>); exact official statuses are recorded separately for each submission route.

Working candidate names use title case. All-uppercase names are reserved for formally assigned Unicode character names and explicit proposal-style repertoire data; a working name does not become official merely because it appears in a proposal. ([U27](Research/Sources.md#u27))
