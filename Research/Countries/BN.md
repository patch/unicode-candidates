Brunei is in both the genus and *D. zibethinus* native-range mappings, has official production statistics, and has strong evidence of national seasonal consumption. Royal Brunei prohibits durian in baggage, but a traceable physical sign is still required.

## Classification

- **Native range:** genus true; *D. zibethinus* true.
- **Cultivation:** established (high confidence).
- **Consumption:** national (high confidence).
- **Signage:** policy-only.
- **2023 fresh imports:** US$7,563,000.
