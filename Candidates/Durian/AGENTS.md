# Durian Candidate Guidance

- Load [Overview.md](Overview.md) for work that affects Durian strategy, status, or project-wide conclusions; for narrower work, begin with the relevant local note.
- The working judgement favours encoding a durian character, particularly because of repeated symbol use on signage in Southeast Asian countries, but research remains open to disconfirming evidence and better interpretations.
- Catalogue the project owner’s existing durian sign-photo collection before commissioning or broadly collecting further sign images.
- Defer the systematic durian plain-text-interchange and counterargument audit until that collection can be shared and assessed.
- Keep durian-specific findings in this candidate folder. Move a finding to the repository-level `Research/` or `Methods/` only when it genuinely applies beyond durian.
