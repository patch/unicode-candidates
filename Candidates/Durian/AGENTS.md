# Durian Candidate Guidance

- Load [Overview.md](Overview.md) for work that affects Durian strategy, status, or project-wide conclusions; for narrower work, begin with the relevant local note.
- The working judgement favours encoding a durian character, particularly because of repeated symbol use on signage in Southeast Asian countries, but research remains open to disconfirming evidence and better interpretations.
- For strategy work, test conclusions against the strongest current non-encoding model and decision-changing evidence in [Overview.md](Overview.md); do not treat functional signage, subject importance, or repertoire fit as a substitute for demonstrated plain-text need.
- Use the completed contributor photo catalogue and [public-safe synthesis](<Research/Contributor photo corpus synthesis.md>) as the baseline before commissioning or broadly collecting further sign images; collect only a named evidential or publication gap.
- The systematic durian plain-text-interchange and counterargument audit is now the next principal Character-route research task. Keep it separate from the later unlabelled form-recognition test.
- Keep durian-specific findings in this candidate folder. Move a finding to the repository-level `Research/` or `Methods/` only when it genuinely applies beyond durian.
