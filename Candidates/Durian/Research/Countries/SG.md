Singapore is in the native genus range through *D. singaporensis* but not the native *D. zibethinus* range. It is an established territory-wide consumer market and has an MRT durian-prohibition photograph licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

## Classification

- **Native range:** genus true; *D. zibethinus* false.
- **Cultivation:** not-identified (low confidence).
- **Consumption:** territory-wide (high confidence).
- **Signage:** verified-photographed.
- **2023 fresh imports:** US$15,911,000.
