## Airbnb house-rules screenshot

One project contact supplied a screenshot of an Airbnb house-rules image. The contact’s identity and the file’s chain of custody are retained privately. Sandy Patch’s photograph permission does not cover the screenshot or the underlying house-rules content.

The private catalogue retains the complete screenshot unchanged and a separate privacy crop. The complete screenshot remains private because it contains interface, account, message, and personal information that is unnecessary to the evidence. For project purposes, a faithful crop containing only the host’s content does not add a separate layer of authorship attributable to the person who supplied the screenshot. Their permission is not required solely on that basis, and public attribution should not name them.

Treat the crop as a low-resolution evidentiary reproduction of the underlying host content. Before publication:

- select only the portion materially needed to document or analyse the rule;
- establish and record the source, creator where known, and publication basis for the underlying content;
- remove the interface, messages, username, and other avoidable personal or account information; and
- confirm that the crop and its presentation do not misrepresent the original context.

The privacy crop is a candidate public derivative, not yet a selected publication asset. A scoped decision may approve it under the project’s non-free evidentiary-use standard without treating the screenshot supplier as an additional rights holder. Sandy may provide corroborating context or a separate capture, but that evidence does not replace the private provenance of this particular file.
