This note publishes safe evidence records and the decision-bearing findings from a privately held contributor corpus. It does not publish the originals, source filenames, exact coordinates, message text, private storage paths, or the full catalogue.

## Corpus and method

The private catalogue contains 165 images captured or saved from 30 March 2025 to 4 June 2026 and consolidated into 91 event groups. Sandy Patch created 164 camera photographs. One further item is a guest-supplied screenshot of an Airbnb house-rules image; the complete screenshot is held privately, and the underlying host content has a separate publication basis.

Every image and supplied message comment received a first-pass review. The event-level audit then examined:

- seven functional event groups containing 18 images, of which 11 directly depict the rule or handling instruction and seven are contextual companions; and
- three adverse, comparative, or mixed event groups containing 20 images.

The audit separated a photographed deployment from its design lineage. Repeated photographs of one sign are one event, and separate deployments of the same printed artwork are not counted as independent designs. The functional signs were coded for whole or cut fruit, silhouette, spikes, stalk, interior detail, colour, wording, prohibition overlay, venue context, likely design relationship, and recognizability limits.

A six-row sequence of first-pass visual descriptions in one multi-image retail event was corrected during the deep review. The source files, identifiers, capture order, and supplied comments were not transposed; only the derived visual descriptions and classifications were affected.

## Form and composition findings

The functional set supports persistence of a generic durian referent across five observed designs:

- a single filled spiky whole fruit;
- paired filled and outlined whole fruits;
- paired jagged-outline whole fruits;
- a detailed colour cut-open fruit with exposed flesh; and
- a monochrome cut-open fruit with interior pods.

Spiky husk or outline and a stalk recur, but neither is invariant across every design at the same level of abstraction. Whole and cut-open forms both occur. The prohibition circle, slash, hand, exact wording, neighbouring rules, operator branding, and sign layout vary or recur as higher-level composition; the corpus does not support treating any of them as part of a separately encoded ‘no durian’ entity.

This variation is evidence against a one-artwork identity. It is not yet proof of a stable abstract character. Every functional occurrence remains embedded in words, an overlay, a venue, or detailed illustration. The audit did not test unlabelled forms at small monochrome sizes, and it did not establish that readers preserve the fruit unit when all of that context is removed.

## Independence and venue breadth

The seven functional groups cover four public-transport deployments, two retail handling-instruction deployments, and one accommodation rule. They reduce to five observed design lineages:

- one unresolved Singapore transit-panel design;
- one Singapore bus design;
- one Sentosa operator design reused on two vehicle types;
- one illustrated retail template photographed at two times and coordinates; and
- one lodging-rules design of unresolved source.

This is broader than repeated photographs of one sign, but it is not five proven cases of independent invention. The project has not traced the commissioning body, icon supplier, or source asset for every design. Reuse within Sentosa and the exact repetition of the retail template must be counted as reuse, not independent convergence.

The corrected soursop comparator is the private item mapped to DUR-EVID-0010; it is not the adjacent freeze-dried-durian photograph that received the comparator description in the first-pass table.

## What the corpus establishes

The audit strengthens three bounded findings:

1. Functional use is not confined to one exact artwork or one venue type.
2. A durian base referent can be recognized across visibly different whole-fruit and cut-fruit designs when the complete sign supplies wording and context.
3. Circle, slash, hand, text, branding, and layout are composition around the fruit rather than evidence for a separate ‘no durian’ character.

It does not establish:

- unaided recognition after wording, overlay, and venue context are removed;
- five independent inventions or five independent organizational source systems;
- a settled representative-glyph range;
- user entry, exchange, quotation, search, indexing, parsing, accessibility, or archival preservation of a durian unit in plain text; or
- a concrete loss when ordinary wording, images, metadata, or higher-level sign systems are used.

The corpus therefore removes the prior ‘unreviewed private collection’ dependency but leaves the principal Character-route question unresolved. It supports a bounded plain-text-interchange and counterargument audit, not proposal drafting.
